✦ Pitch Deck • 1-Page Summary

MediScan AI — SahiDawai

AI-powered packaging scanner that explains any medicine in vernacular Hinglish with audio.

01 ⚠️

The Real Problem

72% of Indian patients cannot read complex English medical jargon on blister packs, causing fatal dosage errors, wrong timings, and complete vulnerability to dangerous WhatsApp forwards.

02 💡

Our Solution

Snap any medicine strip and get instant, empathetic answers in conversational Hinglish (What it is, Uses, Dosage, Cautions) + One-click Audio playback for elderly and non-literate users.

03 🛡️

Privacy & Architecture

Zero-Data Retention by design: On-device WebAssembly OCR (Tesseract.js), clinical validation via live OpenFDA API, and multi-tier neural TTS failover with zero server storage of patient scans.

04 💰

Massive Cost Savings

Empowers families with Jan Aushadhi generic alternatives — saving ordinary households up to 80% on monthly medical bills, transforming healthcare access across Tier-2/Tier-3 India.

Stack: Next.js 16 • WASM OCR • OpenFDA • Neural TTS
Target: 1.4B Indian Citizens
